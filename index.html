About Amigos
Peri-peri chicken is Portuguese-African flame food — marinated birds, fire-grilled, served with the heat you choose.

Amigos Food Hut sits at 10 Albion Street in the heart of Dunstable, a few minutes off the A5 and a short walk from the town square. The kitchen turns out peri-peri chicken to Nando’s-style heat scales, stone-baked pizzas, doner and shish kebabs, burgers, fried chicken, and the usual late-night takeaway favourites.

We’re known locally as Amigo’s — the friend you call when you want flame food without leaving the house. Collect from the counter, or order on Just Eat for delivery anywhere in LU6.
